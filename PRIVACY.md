# Privacy

This plugin only contains configuration. It points your AI client to DangoVault's MCP server at `https://app.dangovault.com/api/v1/mcp`.

- **You decide.** Nothing is shared until you sign in to DangoVault and press **Allow** on the consent screen.
- **Read-only.** The connection can search and read your library (cards, tags, collections, including those shared with you). It cannot save, edit or delete anything, or change your account.
- **You can revoke it.** Disconnect the assistant at any time in DangoVault › Settings › Connected assistants. Unused connections expire after 90 days.
- **Your assistant is not a DangoVault provider.** What it does with what it reads is governed by its own terms.

Full policy: https://app.dangovault.com/privacidad · Contact: soporte@dangovault.com
